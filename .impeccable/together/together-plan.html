Read along — together mode plan

The reader becomes the listening surface. The page stays the room (Appearance ground and ink, Newsreader body); narration moves through it as a soft copper wash on the narrated sentence, with an optional word mark where narration anchors are exact. Controls are the existing player vocabulary — copper play control that morphs circle↔softened square, 30-second skips, speed and sleep — gathered into a quiet tray (phone), a side panel (unfolded), or the lower half (tabletop). Estimated places are never shown as confident: they get a dotted sage underline and “≈”.

The running foot carries chapter, audio time, and status (“≈” when estimated). A centre tap on margins shows the reader's top bar; a tap on text plays from that sentence (keeps play/pause).
