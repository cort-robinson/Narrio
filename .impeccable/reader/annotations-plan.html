Search, highlights, notes, and bookmarks — layout and interaction plan

Workstream F inside the reader chrome from reader-chrome-plan.png. Everything stays out of the way until asked for: search is one top-bar action; highlighting starts from Android's own text-selection toolbar; bookmarks are a ribbon hanging from the page; lists live in the Contents sheet's reserved Bookmarks and Highlights tabs. Highlight colours are four semantic names (yellow, green, blue, pink) with separate Night and Day tints drawn by the navigator at 30% over the page; a note adds an underline in the same colour. Search hits use copper. Narration (workstream E) draws its own decoration group, so neither replaces the other.
