# Narrio artwork

The Secret Garden's bundled fallback cover is original art generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this app. Its exact prompt is in [secret-garden-prompt.txt](secret-garden-prompt.txt) and embedded in the source PNG. The generated output was copied without pixel edits to `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/secret_garden.png`.

The remaining fallback covers are original Compose drawings and typography. Retrieved artwork takes precedence once it loads, and is displayed in its original aspect ratio. Public recordings use Internet Archive artwork; matched book metadata may supply Audible images or Open Library covers. Retrieved artwork belongs to its respective rights holders. It identifies a catalog book and does not certify that an indexed release is the pictured narrated edition. The recording detail links to the metadata source. Metadata providers do not supply playback audio; discovery and delivery remain independent.

Newsreader and Manrope are bundled Google Fonts, used under the SIL Open Font License. Their licenses are included in this directory. Newsreader: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/newsreader. Manrope: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope.
