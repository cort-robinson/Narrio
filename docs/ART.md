# Narrio artwork

The Secret Garden's shipped cover is original art generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this app. Its exact prompt is in [secret-garden-prompt.txt](secret-garden-prompt.txt) and embedded in the source PNG. The generated output was copied without pixel edits to `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/secret_garden.png`.

The remaining covers are original Compose drawings and typography. They deliberately do not impersonate commercial audiobook cover editions. Catalog metadata and audio are supplied by Internet Archive / LibriVox, separately from Narrio's cover design.

Newsreader and Manrope are bundled Google Fonts, used under the SIL Open Font License. Their licenses are included in this directory. Newsreader: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/newsreader. Manrope: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope.
